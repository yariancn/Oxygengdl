  • The right to access, correct, or delete certain personal information.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): You may submit a verifiable consumer request to contact@oxyhyperbaric.com. We will respond within the timeframes required by California law.

To exercise applicable rights, email contact@oxyhyperbaric.com with the subject line “Privacy Request” and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity before processing it.

Our services and websites are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent, in compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us so we can delete it.

Services for minors may be arranged by a parent or legal guardian, who must provide consent and information on the minor’s behalf.
